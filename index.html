Everyday Protections

How We Protect You

Deploying practical, localized safety infrastructure designed around the specific daily patterns of Zambian communities.

01

Safe Crossings Network

Building high-visibility raised crosswalks and speed bumps directly across high-density pedestrian junctions like school zones and open-air markets to aggressively slow oncoming highway traffic.

02

Anti-Corruption Body Cams

Partnering with RTSA and the Zambia Police to equip road check units with continuous-loop body cameras. This stops arbitrary traffic delays, eliminates bribery loops, and ensures transparent data logs.

03

Highway Isolation Fencing

Installing structural divider fences along major routes like Cairo Road or the Great East Road market stretches, preventing unexpected pedestrian darting and establishing clear, safe crossing corridors.

The Golden Hour Threshold

Critical Post-Crash Survival Protocol

Over 50% of serious trauma fatalities occur due to a lack of immediate care within the first hour of an accident. Knowing exactly how to respond preserves lives while dispatch units are in transit.

STEP 1

Secure the Perimeter

Park your vehicle safely well before the scene. Turn on your hazard lights immediately to warn speeding long-distance trucks and oncoming traffic.

STEP 2

Trigger Rapid Dispatch

Call emergency medical links immediately. Clearly relay your highway corridor location, approximate kilometer markers, and total count of injured persons.

STEP 3

Control Heavy Bleeding

Do not move victims unless there is an immediate fire hazard. Apply constant, direct physical pressure to open wounds using clean cloths until medical teams arrive.

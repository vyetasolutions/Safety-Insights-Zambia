Who We Are

Safety Insights Zambia Limited is a leading consulting and technical agency focused exclusively on saving lives on our roads. We partner with government statutory bodies to introduce modern compliance monitoring, evidence-based research, and strict fleet modernization practices.

Our mandate is clear: reduce road fatalities, eliminate corruption on our roads, and empower drivers and officers through comprehensive training.
